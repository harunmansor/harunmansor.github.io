# Dopamine. Sounds familiar ?

I talked about Dopamine in past time where i posted about 6 pages of Whatsapp statuses just talking about Dopamine just because i wanna fill my time rather than just sitting blindly in a 5 hours bus ride to Kuala Lumpur. Dopamine is the thing that is responsible for how many motivation and drive we have in out daily lives. We all have a baseline level of dopamine circulating in our body daily. The level of baseline dopamine depends on the individual. Ali may have slightly higher baseline level of dopamine in his system compared to Shamil. This means Ali may be more motivated in his daily lives compared to Shamil. This baseline level of dopamine can increase and decrease, so when i say "baseline", it doesn't mean that it is static. Andrew Huberman said this in his podcast :

> "If you remember nothing else from this episode, please remember this, when you exprience something really desirable, really exciting to you, very pleasurable... what happens afterwards is your baseline level of dopamine drops, so this peaks "
> Andrew Huberman

So now we know that when we do something pleasurable to us, we experience a peak in the baseline of dopamine, and then after some time, it drops even below the previous baseline level of dopamine. Let's say you have a baseline of 6, the you play games with your friends, you got a peak to level 9. Afterwards, the level will go back underneath 6, it can be 3 or 4 etc. And what happens when the baseline level of dopamine is low in our body ? We become lousy and unmotivated and when we feel these kind of feelings, ou brain will tell us to bring it back to a normal healthy level by forcing us to do pleasurable activities like doom-scrolling, binging Netflix shows and playing games.
